# Summons experiment — is the Duchess too strong? (2026-09-03)

Question (Tyler): with the Duchess you summon her when you're in check and you're just out of it. Should summoning cost a turn?

## Current rules (lib/run/abilities.ts)

| Rule | Today |
|---|---|
| Casting a summon (Squire, Bishop Squire, Page, Twin, Duchess, Dragon, Vanguard) | **Free.** `applySummonAlly` / `applySummonKnight` do not tick the move count or hand the turn over. You cast, then still move Rookie OR the summon. |
| Moving a summon | Costs the turn ("one body per turn", `applyControlledAllyMove`) — except a **T5** Squire / Bishop Squire / Twin, whose move is free once per turn (`allyHasFreeMove`). Duchess and Dragon never get a free move. |
| Duchess | Queen beside Rookie. Lifetime 2/3/4/4/6 enemy turns by tier; charges 1/1/1/2/2. |
| Squire / Bishop Squire / Twin / Vanguard | Charges 1/1/2/2/2. Twin lasts 4/6/8/level/level; Squire 6/9/9/level/level. |
| Dragon | Queen+knight moves, 2/3/4/4/5 turns, 1 charge until T5. |
| Enemies vs summons | Enemies capture summons like any ally; the fleeing king avoids squares they attack. Summon captures stun the king. |

So the "get out of check for free" feeling is real: the cast itself is a free extra body, dropped between Rookie and the attacker (or threatening it), and Rookie still moves. Support cards (Swap / Sacrifice / Knighting) are free actions too.

## What the numbers say

1. **The Duchess is not the outlier — every ability is.** On Fortress hard, Duchess alone wins 79-97% on L8-L10 where no-ability wins 16-26%. But Surge alone and Knight-hop alone win 98-100%, and the plain starter kit (freeze + hop + surge) is 100%. The bot finds Duchess slightly *weaker* than the finishers. Tyler's Duchess-feels-broken read is about the free cast, which is also how the bot uses her (see 3).
2. **Fortress L5 is not hard for the bot (99% with nothing).** Tyler's two losses there are a human-play issue (the level punishes one specific mistake), not a Duchess balance issue; the summon numbers on L5 don't move.
3. **Making the cast end the turn is a real nerf, not a wipe.** Duchess drops 93% -> 70% averaged, and 79% -> 39% on the hardest level (L9); Squire 87 -> 66, Twin/Dragon 93 -> 74. Losses shift to "captured" (the summon no longer saves you from the attack you're already under) and "move-limit" (the cast now eats a move). Duchess T1 (2-turn queen) is already the weakest summon (84%); tier is not the lever.
4. **In a kit the nerf vanishes.** duchess + hop + surge is 100% either way, because Surge and Knight-hop alone already clear these levels. Nerfing summons without touching the finishers won't change how easy Hard feels to a veteran.

Pending: the nightmare pass for all three runs was still running when this was written; expect the same direction (nightmare's king also reacts to ally moves, which should shrink the summon lift a little more).

## Recommendation

**Make casting a summon end Rookie's turn** (the summon appears, enemies move, then next turn you move her or Rookie). One-line change in `applySummonAlly` / `applySummonKnight` (reuse the turn-ending tail of `applyControlledAllyMove`). Expected effect: summon-alone win rates on hard late levels fall ~20-40 points (Duchess ~93% -> ~70%, L9 ~40%), and the "cast her while in check and you're safe" line dies, because the attacker gets its move before she can act. Do NOT also nerf Duchess tiers; T1 is already the weakest summon. Keep summon *moves* as today (they already cost the turn). If the goal is "Hard should feel hard for everyone," the next target is Surge / Knight-hop (100% alone), not the summons.

Harness limits: the bot is an MCTS T5, not a human; it dismisses offers, plays one loadout per cell, and difficulty modes were only measured on hard here. Cells are 100 games, so +/-5 points is noise.
