## Lessons

### The bot under-casts: at 56% of Tyler's 96 verified casts, T5 would have moved instead

Tyler's casts sit on levels he cleared 93% of the time. The bot's rollout policy only jitters abilities into contention (+rng*3); Tyler treats a cast as the MAIN line when enemies are thick (avg 7 enemies on board at cast time).

### Charge discipline: 52% of Tyler's targeted casts land on L7+

Across 23 runs, 35/67 targeted casts happen on L7-L10 where the move-limit pressure is highest. The bot spends charges whenever rollouts jitter them up, with no notion of saving finishers for the levels that need them.

## Tuning shipped off this evidence

Two flag-guarded priors landed in the T5 bot (`--tyler-priors`, default ON; `--tyler-priors=off` restores the untuned bot):

1. **Cast-as-main-line on thick boards** (rollout policy, `bots/mcts.ts`): ability casts get a flat +3 (not just jitter) when 6+ enemies are on the board. Evidence: Tyler casts at avg 6.7-9.5 enemies left, those levels cleared 78-100%, and T5 would have played a plain move at 56% of his 96 verified casts.
2. **Finisher charge discipline** (root pick, `bots/mcts.ts`): on L1-6 with 4+ moves of slack, a one-charge-per-run finisher cast is only chosen if no non-spending candidate is within 10% rollout wins. Evidence: 52% of Tyler's targeted casts land on L7+; the untuned bot spends charges whenever rollouts jitter them up.
