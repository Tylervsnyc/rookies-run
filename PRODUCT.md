# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Phone-first players of Rookie's Revenge (run.chesspath.app + the iOS shell): chess beginners and casual players who came from Chess Path, playing one daily 10-level capture-the-king run in a few minutes, often one-handed, often in a noisy place (gym, commute). They know the pieces, not theory. The job: beat today's run, climb the ladder, unlock powers.

## Product Purpose
A daily chess roguelike: Rookie (a rook) hunts the enemy King through 10 levels, casting abilities drawn from a daily kit. It exists to be the retention loop for Chess Path — a reason to come back tomorrow. Success = players finish the run, feel the win, and return the next day.

## Brand Personality
Fun, over-invested, dramatic. Rookie is an AI who cares too much about your chess: proud and short when you win, a theatrical sore loser when she loses ("This is fine. This is completely fine."). Visually it is a mobile game, not a lesson: Clash-style navy/gold game UI, chunky pressable buttons, painted trading-card ability art. Big moments (level cleared, run won, ability unlocked) are celebrated loudly; failure is funny, never punishing.

## Anti-references
- Chess.com / Lichess density and grey seriousness.
- Corporate SaaS gradients, glass cards, stock "Get Started" energy.
- Kids'-app cartoonishness; adults play this.
- Generic "modal with a headline and a button" popups (the thing being replaced).
- Emojis anywhere in the UI.

## Design Principles
1. Rookie is on your side — every screen carries her voice, short and warm.
2. Show, don't explain — the real board, real pieces, real art; minimal copy.
3. Celebrate everything, even failure — wins are loud, losses are a joke, both are quick.
4. Momentum over perfection — never trap the player; one tap moves forward.
5. Locked palette — navy #0f1c3f / #1c2f63, gold #FFC800, Revenge red #E53935, white text with dark outline, DM Sans only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Tap targets ≥44px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (crossfade fallbacks). Text contrast ≥4.5:1 on the navy surfaces. Works at 360px wide and up.
